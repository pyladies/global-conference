Vicky is a techie, an advocate for diversity in tech, and a QQI certified Creative Trainer. 

Before [PyLadies Dublin](https://dublin.pyladies.com) which she founded in 2013, she was the main organiser for Python Ireland user group for [over 10 years](https://python-ireland-guide.readthedocs.io/en/latest/guide/history.html), chaired the [first four PyCon Irelands](https://python-ireland-guide.readthedocs.io/en/latest/guide/conference/pyconie-committee.html), but nowadays she can be found organising and running [community tech events](https://eventgeek.ie/about) around Dublin, [game jams](https://gamecraft.it) and [advocating diversity in tech](https://diversityintech.fyi), including PyLadies Dublin.

Vicky is also on [PSF Grants Working Group](https://wiki.python.org/psf/GrantsWG/Charter), a PSF Fellow and EPS Fellow, and was [awarded the PSF Community Service Award](https://pyfound.blogspot.com/2021/10/vicky-twomey-lee-awarded-psf-community.html) in 2021.

She got bitten by the Maker advocacy bug after being [the first Maker Advocate with Dublin Maker in 2019](https://dublinmaker.ie/looking-back-as-maker-advocate/); she hopes to bring more art and tech together in events she is organises, and get more people into making and sharing [zines](https://zines.micro.blog/)! She's now a [QQI qualified Creative Trainer](https://www.creativeskillnet.ie/course/the-creative-trainer-2025/) and sparking with ideas after completing her [NCAD's Creative Learning course](https://www.ncad.ie/postgraduate/professional-development-short-courses/professional-certificate-in-creative-learning/).
