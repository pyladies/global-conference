# Workshop guide for PyLadies Conference

## As Conference Participant

### What is a Workshop?

Workshops are live coding sessions lead by workshop facilitators, and attended by 
people who want to learn about a particular topic.
A workshop is an opportunity for learning and self-development with support
from peers (other participants and facilitators).

Please note that the workshops will **not** be recorded.

### How will the workshop take place at PyladiesCon?

The workshop takes place online and live on discord. Interested participants will
choose to join the workshop via discord, there will be announcements on how and when
the workshops will take place.

During the conference, each workshop will have its own Discord channel for interested
participants to join.

### What do I need to participate in the workshop?

1. Secure your conference ticket (please check [PyLadiesCon website](https://2026.conference.pyladies.com) for updates).
1. Once ticket is registered, join PyLadiesCon Discord server and follow the instructions.
1. Join the workshop you are interested on discord. This information will be announced on discord.


### When and Where?

PyLadiesCon 2026 runs from 5-7 December, and workshops will be scheduled between **5 December 2026 at 16:30 UTC and 6 December 2026 at 23:45 UTC** throughout the conference before the Sprints. The final schedule will be published before the event.


## As Workshop Organizer
* You will have received an invitation to the [PyLadiesCon Portal](https://portal.pyladies.com)
    - If you have, please accept your invititation
    - If not, you can start by submitting your proposal here: [https://conference.pyladies.com/2026-speaker-portal/](https://conference.pyladies.com/2026-speaker-portal/)
* You can edit your bio and session details via the [PyLadiesCon Portal](https://portal.pyladies.com)
* We can add additional presenters to your workshop via [PyLadiesCon Portal](https://portal.pyladies.com), please send us the following so we can send an invitation to your co-presenter:
    - Full name
    - Contact email 

* If there's pre-requisites for participants to prepare before attending the workhop, please update the workshop session through the [PyLadiesCon Portal](https://portal.pyladies.com) with the workshop details, and upload an required resources if required.

* Let PyLadiesCon Organizers know:
    - How many participants you can manage
    - If you need any extra volunteers to assist you 

## Code of Conduct

As with everything at PyLadiesCon, your participation in the workshop must follow the
[PyLadiesCon Code of Conduct](https://2026.conference.pyladies.com/en/coc/).
