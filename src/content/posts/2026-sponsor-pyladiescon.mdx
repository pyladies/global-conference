## Sponsorship for PyLadiesCon 2026 is open

PyLadiesCon 2026 takes place the first weekend of December, fully online on Discord, and we are
now looking for sponsors to make it happen. The
[sponsorship packages](https://2026.conference.pyladies.com/en/sponsors/) are live, with tiers
from $750 to $10,000 plus a custom option.

This year is a special edition. Instead of a talks-only program, we are focusing on workshops,
panels, community activities, and PyJam, our pre-recorded performance sessions. The goal is
knowledge transfer: giving PyLadies around the world practical skills and a place to meet each
other.

PyLadiesCon is run entirely by volunteers. Sponsorship covers the services that make a global,
multilingual online conference possible, and it funds the PyLadies mission beyond the event
itself.

## Why sponsor PyLadiesCon

PyLadiesCon reaches a global, technical audience that is hard to find anywhere else. In 2025,
906 people attended from 72 countries, up from 732 in 2024 and 600+ in 2023. About a third were
engineers, over a quarter were students or interns, and roughly 10% were scientists. Experience
levels ran from student to executive, with the largest groups at student, mid-level, and junior.

The top countries were the United States, Germany, India, the United Kingdom, the Netherlands,
Ghana, Nigeria, Canada, and Ireland, with 64 more countries making up the rest. If your team
hires remotely or wants to be visible to early-career and mid-career Python developers worldwide,
this is that room.

Your sponsorship also supports PyLadies' work year-round, beyond the conference itself: financial
aid for community members to attend Python events, support for regional PyLadies gatherings
around the world, equipment grants for PyLadiesCon speakers, and the Outstanding PyLady Award.

As a sponsor you also get direct access to attendees through dedicated Discord channels, a live
Sponsors AMA, job listings on the website, and shoutouts across our social media and blog.

## Sponsorship packages

There are six tiers, and every one of them includes a Custom Discord Role, your name on the
website, and mentions on our social media. Here is what each adds on top of that.

| Tier | Price (USD) | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Champion](https://2026.conference.pyladies.com/en/sponsors/) | $10,000 | Keynote intro video, technical talk slot, sponsor panel seat, brand visibility during breaks, opening/closing shoutout, dedicated Discord channel, email blast, logo, blog write-up, 6 LinkedIn posts, job listing, Sponsors AMA |
| [Supporter](https://2026.conference.pyladies.com/en/sponsors/) | $6,000 | Technical talk slot, sponsor panel seat, brand visibility during breaks, opening/closing shoutout, dedicated Discord channel, logo, blog write-up, 4 LinkedIn posts, job listing, Sponsors AMA |
| [Connector](https://2026.conference.pyladies.com/en/sponsors/) | $3,000 | Opening/closing shoutout, dedicated Discord channel, logo, blog write-up, 2 LinkedIn posts, job listing, Sponsors AMA |
| [Booster](https://2026.conference.pyladies.com/en/sponsors/) | $1,500 | Dedicated Discord channel, logo, 1 LinkedIn post, job listing; Sponsors AMA available for +$100 |
| [Partner](https://2026.conference.pyladies.com/en/sponsors/) | $750 | Logo, 1 LinkedIn post, job listing; Sponsors AMA available for +$100 |
| [Individual](https://2026.conference.pyladies.com/en/sponsors/) | $50+ | Name on the website and social media thanks; Sponsors AMA available for +$100 |

Here are some highlighted benefits:

- **Technical Talk Slot** (Champion and Supporter): a 15-minute talk plus 5 minutes of Q&A,
  scheduled once the program is announced.
- **Panel Discussion** (Champion and Supporter): a roughly 35-minute sponsor panel on a topic of
  your choice, placed in the timezone with the most demand. These are consistently the most
  popular sessions of the conference.
- **Sponsors AMA on Discord**: a live Q&A your team hosts on our server. Attendees ask about your
  company, your open roles, or the tech you work with.

If none of these fit exactly, we are happy to put together a **custom sponsorship**. It is our
most requested option. Pick the benefits that match your goals and we will build a package
around them.

Full details are on the [sponsors page](https://2026.conference.pyladies.com/en/sponsors/), and
you can also download the
[sponsorship prospectus](https://www.canva.com/design/DAHWBzuj5PQ/N-aBUGrv93cb3YcxBv8vzA/view?utlId=hdd4a40cd54).

## How to get involved

To sponsor, email us at [sponsors@pyladies.com](mailto:sponsors@pyladies.com) and tell us which
tier interests you or what you would like a custom package to include. A member of our
sponsorship team will get back to you soon. Sponsoring early means more time for your brand to be
visible in the lead-up to the conference.

You do not need a company budget to help. Individual sponsorships start at $50, and you can also
[donate](https://2026.conference.pyladies.com/en/donate) any amount to support the conference and
PyLadies.

If your employer has a sponsorship or developer relations budget, forward this post to the person
who manages it. And if you know a company that should be in front of nearly a thousand
Pythonistas from more than 70 countries, please share this with them.

We will see you online the first weekend of December.
