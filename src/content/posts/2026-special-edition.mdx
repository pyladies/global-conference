## Save the date 🗓️

PyLadiesCon is back, and this one is a little different.

**December 5–7, 2026. 100% online, on Discord. Free, as always.**

Details are taking shape at [2026.conference.pyladies.com](https://2026.conference.pyladies.com).

## What's different this year

For the last three years, PyLadiesCon has been organized as a conference of talks, panels, and keynotes.
Each year, we hosted approximately 6 keynotes, 30+ speakers, 4-6 panel sessions, and various workshops.

This year's edition is built around **knowledge transfer** rather than talks and keynotes.
Our program will consist of:

- **Workshops**: hands-on sessions where you get to learn and build a thing.
- **Panels**: conversations between community members and leaders about topics affecting our global community.
- **PyJam**: A showcase of performances from our community members.

## Why we made this change

There are various reasons that led us to this decision. We will be publishing a more thorough
retrospective about all of the contributing factors, however we wanted to reassure our community
that we did not make this decision lightly. Each of the PyLadiesCon core team members took
everything into consideration in deciding on this year's format.

This does not mean that PyLadiesCon will forever change into this format. This simply means we are
always trying to find the best ways to serve our community. We have done the same similar format for
three years in a row. This change gives us the opportunity to serve our community in a slightly
different way, but not a drastic change.

## Who this is for

PyLadiesCon has always been open to anyone who supports our mission, regardless of their gender
identity, and whether or not there's a PyLadies chapter in your city. Over the past few years, we've
heard from people in other underrepresented groups in tech who want to be part of it. You're welcome
here. This edition is designed with you in mind too.

## What's next

Mark your calendar: **December 5–7, 2026**, online. Add it now, while you're thinking about it,
and then tell the people you'd want to be there with you: your local PyLadies chapter, your team,
the friend who has been meaning to get into Python. It's free and it's fully online, so the only
thing anyone needs is the date.

Follow us on social media to catch announcements as they happen. We're on
[Mastodon](https://fosstodon.org/@pyladiescon),
[Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/pyladiescon.bsky.social),
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/pyladiescon), and
[Instagram](https://instagram.com/pyladiescon). Subscribe to the **#PyLadiesCon** hashtag too, so
you see what the rest of the community is sharing, not just what we post.

Important news will always be published on [our blog](https://conference.pyladies.com/blog) first:
the program, how to take part, and everything else as it's confirmed. If you'd rather not depend on
a social media algorithm to show it to you, subscribe to our
[RSS feed](https://conference.pyladies.com/rss.xml) and it will come to you.

And keep an eye on [2026.conference.pyladies.com](https://2026.conference.pyladies.com). The new
site is still taking shape, so check back as more of it appears.

See you in December.
